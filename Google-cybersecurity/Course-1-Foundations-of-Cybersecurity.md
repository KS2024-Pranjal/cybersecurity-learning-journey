# Course 1 — Foundations of Cybersecurity

## Overview

I completed the first course of the Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate.

This course introduced me to the foundations of cybersecurity and helped me understand the importance of protecting systems, networks, devices, and data.

## Topics I Learned

- What cybersecurity is
- Importance of cybersecurity
- Security risks and threats
- Security controls
- Security frameworks
- Security teams and their responsibilities
- Common cybersecurity concepts
- Importance of protecting information and systems
- Cybersecurity career paths

## Key Learning

I learned that cybersecurity involves protecting information and systems from unauthorized access, damage, disruption, and other security threats.

I also learned about the role of cybersecurity professionals and how security practices help organizations protect their data and technology.

## My Takeaway

This course gave me a foundation for understanding cybersecurity and motivated me to continue developing practical cybersecurity skills.
